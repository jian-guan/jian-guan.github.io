Hello, I'm

Jian Guan

Ph.D. Candidate · MIT EAPS

I study what drives changes in stratospheric composition, the chemical processes behind them, and how they affect climate. My work spans detecting human influence on the ozone layer, constraining organic aerosol photolysis, and quantifying warming from methane-derived stratospheric water vapor.

At MIT, I work with Prof. Susan Solomon, combining satellite and aircraft observations with chemistry–climate models and statistical detection and attribution to test mechanisms and improve model representations.
