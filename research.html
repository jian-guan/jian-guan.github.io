Research

My research asks how the composition of the stratosphere is changing, and how those changes feed back on climate — from detecting the earliest human fingerprints on the ozone layer, to tracking the fate of aerosols and photochemistry in the stratosphere, to constraining the radiative forcing of stratospheric water vapor. Across these projects, a common thread is using satellite observations to test and improve chemistry–climate models such as CESM.
